# R1-VAMS — research project notes for Claude

Two conference papers from one simulation study of merit-based sortition
(Kruijssen et al. 2024; deployed on the Allora network, Pfeffer et al. 2025). Everything that
produces a number lives in `paper/code/`; `paper/README.md` maps every script to the section
it feeds.

## Goal and state (as of 2026-11-20)

Both papers are **complete drafts**: no `\pending{}`, clean builds (no errors, overfull boxes or
undefined references), every number regenerated by a script.

- **Paper A** — `paper/paperA/main.tex`, *Score Before You Select*, 9 pages. Theorem 1
  (pivotality): the bias of scoring the newly selected set (M2) equals
  `(1/N') sum_j g(u_j)`, `g(u)=E[eps 1{eps>u}]` at each participant's flip point — exact for
  any noise law. Its *sign* is the "optimistic choosing" case of Shin, Ramdas & Rinaldo
  (NeurIPS 2019), which the paper credits; the magnitude formula, the log-only audit
  (Section 5) and the noise-to-spread result (damage ~0 at V/sigma_Q<=1, 34-50% at 2, 64-80%
  at 4) are the new parts. VAMS equivalent to static P; headroom <= 0.002.
- **Paper B** — `paper/paperB/main.tex`, *Optimism Must Decay with Staleness*, 7 pages, 2
  figures. Final grid 489 configs, no pick on a boundary (natural limits: floor 0/1, gamma 1,
  tau 1000=Nepochs, TS scale 0, SW-UCB c 0). Stationary: all rules beat tuned static by
  6.3-16.6% of gap. Drift: UCB loses 7/8; D-UCB never loses. Drift-agnostic D-UCB: 4W/0L/8eq,
  mean regret 12.0 vs 13.5. Whitewashing: undefended bandits lose; D-UCB + newcomer prior
  n0=30 never worse than static at any attack strength, better at 20%.

Packaged deliverables: `deliverables/` (PDFs + Overleaf zips), rebuilt by `paper/build_all.sh`.

## Rules that hold every number together

- **Quality = M1** (`run()[5]`), never index 0 (M2, biased) — this is Paper A's whole point.
- **Tune on TUNE seeds, report on disjoint EVAL seeds, paired.** Never pick and report on the
  same seeds. Holm across all comparisons; "equivalent" = TOST at eps=0.005.
- **No tuned pick on a grid boundary** (natural limits excepted); extend the grid instead.
- **Never cite** anything in `paper/code/_pre_audit_2026-10-24/` or `VAMS_paper_v2.docx`.
  `memory_drift.py` is superseded too (in-sample picks).
- **Engine changes**: `engine_regress.py` before/after, 0 differences allowed. As of
  2026-11-20 the engine adds whitewashing (`adv_frac`, `adv_Q`, `prior_n0`, `stats`) and Paper
  A's `noise=` / `stats={'pivot': True}`; regression 0/104 differ against the pre-change engine.
- Tables in `paper/paper*/tables/` are generated (`paperB_tables.py`, `pivotality_report.py`):
  edit the scripts, not the tables.
